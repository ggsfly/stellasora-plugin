👽 Preset Codes
Stella Sora Preset Codes (as of August 20, 2026)
NOTE: You can download or make a copy of this document for your own use.
This document serves as an extension of the Potential Builds section of the Mistique's Field Reports (Stella Sora INFODOC) spreadsheet to allow people to copy preset codes properly. Keep in mind that the document is subject to constant updates whenever new teams are discovered and if there is a need to update existing ones.
Right now, this is only a compilation of preset codes and is just ordered based on the Limited Trekker release. There are plans to categorize the teams based on effectiveness for Boss Blitz/Menace Arena/Finale Echoing but it will take time to think of a proper format for it.
________________


How do I make sense of the Preset Codes?
As of 7/7/2026, Morph’s Record Builder has been integrated for some preset codes. You may click on the preset code to open a link to an image showcasing each unit’s Potential priority (if it has one).


For the potential levels listed in the build, these are their label designations:
* Level 1: Safe to not upgrade past the base level
* Level 2: Upgrade as much as you can, if you are able to
* Level 6: Mandatory to max level
To import a preset code, navigate to the Go button at the bottom right of the game screen then find Preset. After which, you can find the Import Preset button at the top right corner.
